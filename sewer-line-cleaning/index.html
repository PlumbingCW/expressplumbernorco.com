Main sewer backup: do this while you wait

When the main sewer line is blocked, every drain in the house can back up. The lowest fixture usually fills first, often a shower or floor drain.

Stop using water. Locate your cleanout, which is usually a capped pipe in the yard or near the house, and keep the area clear. Do not open the cap while the line is full.

What we do on a sewer line cleaning visit

We open the cleanout and use a larger cable machine than a household drain job needs. The cutting head clears roots and debris. If the blockage is grease or scale, we may switch to water jetting.

After the line flows, we camera it when needed to see whether the clog was a one-time event or a symptom of damage. The camera leads us to the right next step.

Sewage backup: first steps for your household

These steps limit damage and keep the family safe until the plumber arrives.

  • Stop all water use in the house.
  • Keep people and pets out of the wet area.
  • Move rugs and electronics out of reach.
  • Switch off power to wet areas only if you can do it safely.
  • Call us and tell us where the cleanout is.

If the water is high near outlets or an appliance, stay away and call us from a dry area.

Keeping a main line clear in Norco

Where trees stand near a lateral, roots seek water. A small crack in an older line gives roots a way in. If you have had two backups in two years, ask for a camera check instead of another cable pass.

Hard water at about 120 to 185 mg/L in city reports also leaves scale. A water softener can slow it, and a yearly check on risky lines helps.

The cleanout is the most useful fitting in the house

A cleanout is a capped pipe that gives a plumber direct access to the main line. With it, we can clear the line from outside without pulling a toilet. Without it, the work takes longer and costs more.

If you cannot find yours, we can locate it or install one. It is a small job that pays for itself the first time the line backs up. Keep the area around the cap clear of soil, plants and stored items.

Habits that keep a main line open

Wipes labeled flushable do not break down like paper, and they cause many main line blockages. Grease poured down the sink cools and sticks inside the pipe. Roots follow moisture into small cracks and grow.

Keep wipes out of the toilet, wipe pans before washing and ask for a camera look if you have had two backups in two years.

Code and permits

Cleaning a sewer line needs no permit in most cases. Repairs that follow, such as a new cleanout, are governed by the UPC-based California Plumbing Code.

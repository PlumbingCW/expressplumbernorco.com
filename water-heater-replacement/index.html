When a failed tank needs replacing today

A leaking tank cannot be repaired, and the water damage grows every hour. Shut off the cold water at the valve above the heater, turn off the power or gas, and call us.

We have no set arrival window to promise, but we will tell you honestly what the schedule looks like when you call. Many tank swaps take a few hours once the plumber is on site.

How a replacement goes, start to finish

  1. Assess

    We check the old unit, venting, gas or electric supply and the space.

  2. Swap

    We drain the tank, disconnect it, set the new one and reconnect.

  3. Test

    We check for leaks, venting, temperature and the relief valve.

Tank or tankless: which fits your home

Most failed-tank calls end with a like-for-like swap because it is the fastest route to hot water. If you want a change, we can discuss it on the call.

Traditional tank

  • Lower purchase price.
  • Simpler swap in the same spot.
  • Limited by tank size.
  • Commonly 8 to 12 years.

Tankless

  • Hot water on demand.
  • Wall-mounted, saves floor space.
  • Needs the right gas line and venting.
  • Often 20 years with flushing.

Our tankless water heater page covers sizing, venting and flushing in detail.

California rules that apply to the swap

California Health and Safety Code section 19211 says new and replacement water heaters, and existing residential ones, must be braced, anchored or strapped to resist earthquake motion. The code covers units of 120 gallons or less that have a pre-engineered strapping kit. We strap every unit we set.

Permit rules for water heater replacement vary by city. Ask the City of Norco building department what applies at your address, and ask us what we will handle. You can check any plumber on the state contractor board lookup. Our Norco Hills page lists the heater facts that apply to newer hillside homes.

Why Norco tanks often fail sooner

Water from Norco taps comes from purchased treated groundwater and from city wells. The city reports hardness averaging about 122 mg/L in the 2022 data year and about 185 mg/L in the 2021 data year. Hard water builds scale, and scale insulates the burner or elements from the water.

An anode rod that is never replaced is the other common cause. If your tank is more than ten years old and you have never had it flushed, plan the replacement before it fails.

Before the plumber arrives

A quick prep makes the swap faster.

  • Clear a path to the water heater and a few feet around it.
  • Know where the cold supply valve and the gas shutoff are.
  • Move stored items, paint cans and pet beds away from the unit.
  • Take a photo of the label with the model, size and fuel type.
  • Tell us if the unit sits in a garage, closet or attic, since that changes access.

Code and permits

A replacement heater is installed to the California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and often needs a permit. California also requires water heaters to be braced or strapped, which we include.

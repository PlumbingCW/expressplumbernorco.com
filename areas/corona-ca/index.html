Corona by housing age, from the Circle outward

Corona was founded in 1886 as South Riverside and took the name Corona in 1896. Its citrus past and the circular Grand Boulevard shaped the original townsite. Homes near that historic core are older than the neighborhoods that spread outward later.

Older streets often have older drains and supply lines. Newer neighborhoods have newer pipe. We ask the age of your home first, because that sets what we expect to find in the walls and under the yard.

What Corona water does to pipes and parts

The City of Corona reported average hardness of 157 mg/L, or 9.2 grains per gallon, in its 2024 consumer confidence report on 2023 data. The supply mixed city wells at 45.8%, Lake Mathews water from the Colorado River at 49.7% and State Water Project water at 4.5%.

The city also adds ammonia to make chloramines, which last longer in the pipes. Chloramines can wear rubber parts such as toilet flappers faster. If a toilet runs, see toilet repair.

Water heater strapping in Corona homes

California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquakes. Older Corona homes sometimes have missing or loose straps. When we service or replace a heater, we check the strapping and tell you what we find.

It is a small item that matters in a quake. Ask us if you are not sure what your heater has.

Older sewer laterals in Corona neighborhoods

Streets near the historic core of Corona were built long before plastic pipe. Clay and cast iron laterals were common in older eras, and roots find their joints. If a drain in an older home clogs twice in a year, a camera inspection is worth the price.

We give you the video, and we explain what the pipe looks like inside. Then you can choose between cleaning, lining and replacement with real information.

Code, permits and who to ask

Plumbing in Corona follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Permit rules are set by the City of Corona, so we tell you what applies to your address before work starts.

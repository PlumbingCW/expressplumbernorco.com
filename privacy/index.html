This page explains what information we handle when you use this website or call our line. We keep it short because there is little to say.

Calls

When you call, we collect your phone number and the details you give us about the job, such as your address and the problem. We use that to schedule and complete the work. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

This website

This website has no forms and does not collect personal information through the page. It does not use advertising cookies or analytics scripts that we control.

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and some pages show an embedded Google Map. Those requests go to Google and are covered by its own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with people who need it to complete your job, such as a supplier we order a part from, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you or to ask us to delete it, email contact@expressplumbernorco.com or call (951) 467-1450. Updated November 13, 2026.

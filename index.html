Emergency plumber in Norco

What happens when you call about an emergency

A plumbing emergency does not wait for office hours. If a pipe bursts at 2 a.m. or the main drain backs up on a holiday, you want a plumber on the phone fast. Call and describe the problem, and we will tell you what the next step looks like.

When you call, we ask what you see, where the water shutoff is and how old the home is. That short conversation tells us what to bring. It also lets us give you steps to limit damage before anyone arrives.

Not every problem is an emergency, and we say so plainly. A slow drip under a sink can wait for a daytime visit. Water spraying from a wall, sewage in a tub or a gas smell near a water heater cannot wait, and those are the calls to make right away.

  1. Call us

    Dial (951) 467-1450. We ask what is happening and where.

  2. Shut off and stay safe

    We walk you through closing the main valve and keeping people away from hazards.

  3. Ask about the job

    Before you agree to work, ask what the job involves and what it costs.

  4. Fix and confirm

    We repair the problem, test it and explain what we found.

Local water and homes

What Norco water and housing mean for your plumbing

Plumbing problems in Norco have local causes. The city reports moderately hard to hard water, which leaves scale inside heaters and on fixtures. The city also reports chlorine, which wears rubber parts over time. Older homes can add old pipe and fittings to that mix. All plumbing work in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

The table below shows what the sources report and why each fact matters to a homeowner. Test your tap and ask your water utility for its latest annual report, because your street can differ from a system average.

Sewer backup: what to do first

A main line that backs up into a floor drain or tub is a plumbing emergency, and hydro jetting is one of the fastest ways to restore flow.

Stop using water in the house. Do not flush, run the dishwasher or start laundry. Keep children and pets away from standing sewage, and call us with the location of your cleanout if you know it.

What hydro jetting does that a cable cannot

A cable cuts a channel through a clog. Hydro jetting sends water through a hose and a nozzle that sprays back and forward, scrubbing the whole pipe wall. Grease, scale and silt wash away instead of being pushed along.

It also clears fine roots near the opening, though it does not repair a cracked or collapsed pipe. We run a camera first to see what we are cleaning and what we are not.

How a jetting visit runs

  1. Camera

    We scope the line to see roots, scale, sags and breaks.

  2. Access

    We open the cleanout and place the hose and nozzle.

  3. Jet

    We work from the low point outward and flush debris to the main.

  4. Recheck

    A second camera pass confirms the line is clear.

When jetting is not the answer

We recommend against jetting on pipe that is cracked, offset or collapsing, because pressure can worsen a weak spot. If the camera shows a break, you need repair or replacement and we will explain the options.

Older cast iron pipe with heavy corrosion also needs care. We tell you what we see before we choose a method.

Why hard water matters for jetting in Norco

City of Norco reports put average water hardness between roughly 120 and 185 mg/L in recent data years. Minerals that scale pipe walls give grease and hair something to catch on.

Jetting removes that layer, and a softener helps keep it from returning. Ask us whether your line scale looks mild or heavy on the camera.

Restaurants, shops and multi-unit buildings

Grease lines and shared building drains are the classic jetting jobs. A cable opens a hole through grease. A jet cleans the pipe wall so the grease has nothing to cling to. Many food businesses schedule jetting at set intervals instead of waiting for a backup.

If you manage a building, tell us which units back up first. That tells us where the line is restricted and where to place the cleanout access.

What to expect afterward

A freshly jetted line drains fast and quiet. If the camera showed roots or a defect, the clean pipe is also the best time to see what repair is needed. We show you the second camera pass so you see the same pipe we do.

A clean line is not permanent. Grease from the kitchen and wipes in the toilet bring clogs back. A few habits keep the line clear much longer.

Code and permits

Jetting changes no piping, so it sits outside most permit rules. Any repair we find along the way, such as a replaced section, falls under the UPC-based California Plumbing Code.

What to tell us when you call

A few details help us understand the problem.

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • What you see: water, smell, sound or no water at all.
  • Whether you can reach the main shutoff valve.
  • The age of the home and of the water heater if it is involved.
  • A good number to reach you if the call drops.

Email is for non-urgent questions

Email works for questions that can wait for a reply. If water is spraying, sewage is backing up or you smell gas near an appliance, call instead.

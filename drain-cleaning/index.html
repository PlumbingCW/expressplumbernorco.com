When a clogged drain turns into an emergency

A slow sink is a nuisance. Water rising in a tub while the toilet gurgles is an emergency, because the blockage is in a shared line.

While you wait, stop running water and flushing toilets. Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a drain that is already full, since it sits in the pipe and burns skin if a plumber has to work on it. Move rugs and anything electric away from the wet floor.

Signs the clog is in the main line

Match what you see to where the problem probably sits. It saves time on the phone and helps us bring the right equipment.

Several fixtures slow at once

One slow sink is a branch clog. A slow tub, sink and toilet together point to the main drain.

Gurgling in the toilet

Air pushed back up the pipe when you drain the washer or run a sink.

Water in the lowest drain

A shower or floor drain that fills first is closest to the blockage.

A smell that returns

Sewer odor after a drain has been cleared suggests buildup further down the line.

How we clear a drain without guessing

We start by finding out which line is blocked. A single kitchen sink usually means grease, food or a failing trap arm. A hair-and-soap mat is common in bath drains. Roots or a belly in the pipe are more likely when the main line keeps coming back.

For household clogs we use a drain machine, which is a motor-driven cable with a cutting head. The cable breaks up the blockage and pulls it out. Hand augers work for sinks and tubs. When a cable will not hold the line open, we suggest a camera inspection of the sewer line before we recommend more cleaning.

Norco water and the drains it feeds

Norco buys most of its drinking water as treated groundwater, and the city reported about 83% purchased supply in 2023. The same city reports show average hardness between roughly 120 and 185 mg/L as calcium carbonate in recent years. Hard water leaves mineral scale on pipe walls, and scale catches soap and hair.

That does not mean every clog is a water problem. It does mean older drains with rough walls clog faster than new ones. If you see white crust around fixtures, a water softener can slow the buildup that feeds repeat clogs.

What to try before you call, and what to leave alone

A few safe steps are worth trying first.

  • Pour hot tap water slowly into a slow kitchen drain and run it for a minute.
  • Pull the stopper in a tub or bathroom sink and remove hair by hand with a plastic strip.
  • Use a plunger with a good seal, covering the overflow opening with a wet rag.
  • Skip chemical openers, which can damage older pipe and make later work unsafe.
  • Never run a rented machine into a main line if you are unsure where the cleanout is.

If water stays high after two plunger rounds, stop and call. Forcing a blockage deeper can turn a ten-minute fix into a main line job.

Code and permits

Drain and trap work in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). We clean lines without altering traps or vents, so most drain cleaning needs no permit.

Pricing factors

What affects the price of drain cleaning

Drain work is priced by access and effort, not by the minute on site. Insurance rarely pays for a simple clog, though a sewage backup that damages floors may be covered by some policies, so ask your insurer.
